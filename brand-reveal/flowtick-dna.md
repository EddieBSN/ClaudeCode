## Name and casing

"flowtick" — always lowercase in the lockup and wordmark [F:1:14, F:1:21,
N:wwd-1]. Sentence-cased "Flowtick" appears only in running text
("(c) Flowtick. All rights reserved." [F:170:580]). Touchpoints:
flowtick.co, @theflowtick [F:169:568, F:169:571]. Positioning: "Business
Workflow Optimization" [N:root callout] — fixes chaotic processes for
young founders; "No theory. No fluff." [N:wwd-1].

## Mark construction

An 8-lobe scalloped cloud badge with a wave-tick knocked out of the
center (transparent — the field shows through) [F:82:154 "Exclude 1",
F:1:8, F:1:18]. Two overlaid 4-lobe quatrefoils form the 8 scallops
[code F:1:3 — two stacked 105x105 vectors]. The cutout is one smooth
stroke that rises, dips and kicks up at the end — a wave that is also a
tick ("flow" + "tick"); the course modules literally name everything a
Flow [N:Modules DB]. Exact geometry at badge = 105 units [code F:1:3]:
wave centerline bbox x 26..80 (width 54), y 46..60 (amplitude 14), stroke
6, round caps. Scallop notch depth measured from the 443 px master render:
notch radius / outer radius = 0.875 [F:82:154].

## Texture and grain language

Flat. Every brand master is a solid, untextured field with the white
logo [F:1:11, 1:14, 1:18, 1:21, 82:138]. Documents are calm editorial
paper: hairline rules, letterspaced uppercase micro-labels ("FLOWTICK .
WEEKLY KPI TRACKER . TEMPLATE 02" [F:169:570]), soft rounded cards. No
gradients, no glow, no noise anywhere in the identity.

## Energy level

Low and assured. The product sells calm: audits, systems, "scale without
chaos" [N:wwd-3]. Layouts breathe; nothing shouts. The loudest the brand
gets is a muted indigo chip.

## Recurring motifs

The wave/flow line (the mark's cutout, the "Flow" module naming); the
scalloped badge; lowercase everything; timeline dots and stepped flows in
docs [N:wwd-2 distribution row, N:wwd-4 call structure].

## What the brand avoids

Gradients and neon; visual noise and glitch aesthetics (its whole promise
is removing chaos); hard corporate blue + suit imagery; uppercase
shouting; decoration without function.

## Frame 5 treatment decision

Bespoke: a calm laminar flow-field — thin white-blue streamlines flowing
as one wave across a deep Flowtick-navy field, bunching above and below
and parting into a calmer clearing behind the lockup, with the frame-wide
current echoing the mark's rise-dip-kick wave. One line of justification:
Flowtick's identity is flat, deep-navy calm built around a single wave
motif, so the background makes that one motif the hero at frame scale —
stipple, airbrush and glitch energy would all contradict the brand's
anti-chaos promise. Subtle film grain only (composition rule), kept an
order quieter than the Part 1 frames.

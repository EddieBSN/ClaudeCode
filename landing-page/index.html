02 · Why Meridian

Built for the way finance actually closes.

Spreadsheets reconcile a snapshot. Meridian reconciles a living system: every charge, credit, refund, and payout, continuously, with the paper trail attached.

/01

Continuous reconciliation

Every processor payout is matched to its invoices the moment it lands. Month end starts at 99.9% done instead of zero.

/02

Revenue recognition

ASC 606 and IFRS 15 schedules are generated from contract terms, not typed into a workbook. Deferrals update themselves.

/03

Anomaly alerts

Duplicate charges, silent failed renewals, and currency drift get flagged the day they happen, not the week you close.

/04

Multi-entity consolidation

Roll up subsidiaries with different currencies and calendars into one consolidated view, with eliminations handled inline.

/05

Immutable audit trail

Every adjustment carries who, when, and why. Your auditors get read access and stop emailing you for screenshots.

/06

Native exports

Journal entries post to NetSuite, Xero, or QuickBooks with one review step. CSV and API access for everything else.
